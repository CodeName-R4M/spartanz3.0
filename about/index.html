What is SPARTANZ 3.0?

SPARTANZ 3.0 is the flagship department symposium organized by the CSE — Cyber Security Department of New Prince Shri Bhavani College of Engineering, in association with RootSec Club. It brings together students, tech enthusiasts, and cybersecurity professionals for a day of intense competition, knowledge sharing, and innovation.

Inspired by the precision, speed, and strategy of elite motorsport, SPARTANZ 3.0 frames every challenge as a race — where quick thinking, technical skill, and teamwork determine who reaches the podium. From competitive programming to cybersecurity CTFs, from creative design to gaming tournaments, there is a challenge for every kind of competitor.

Vision

Foster and nurture students, inspiring them to be Cyber Security entrepreneurs, by offering a viable and research-centric educational platform for building a robust, resilient, and attack-free digital society.

Mission

  • Abet students to be Cyber Security Entrepreneurs with hands-on, research-based learning for a secure digital society.
  • Strengthen collaborative project learning to address global challenges whilst preserving human and ethical values.
  • Encourage students to help society restore human safety and security in the digital world.

Theme: F1 Racing Inspired

The F1 racing theme is a visual metaphor for what SPARTANZ 3.0 represents: precision engineering, split-second decision making, strategic teamwork, and the pursuit of peak performance. Just as every millisecond counts on the track, every line of code, every flag captured, and every design decision matters in this symposium.
